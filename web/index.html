Circuit Copilot

Describe the logic as expressions and Copilot compiles it to NAND gates in your browser, then checks the result on every possible input before you can tape it out. Use it to write a new reward policy for the vault, or any circuit at all. Prefer plain English? The CLI asks Claude to write the spec: node copilot/cli.mjs "a 2-bit comparator".

Expression cheat sheet

+ - * (minus stops at 0) · & | ^ ~ · == != < <= > >= · ! && || · c ? a : b · << >> by a constant · min() max() bit(x,i) popcount(). Inputs are packed in order, least-significant bit first. At most 16 input bits.

Vault owner · change the policy

Tape out a policy-shaped circuit first, then propose its id. It becomes active after the 2-day timelock, and stakers can see it coming.

Tape out a circuit on OpenGate

Paste a design from Circuit Copilot (out/<name>.json) or a raw netlist. The app mints the NAND transistors you're missing, tapes the circuit out, and you become a builder, which adds +1 to your tier.
